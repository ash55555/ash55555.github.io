Terms, refunds and privacy

Last updated September 2026. Plain-language summary of how booking and payment work.

Who this is with

Ash Tabletop is a paid Dungeons & Dragons game-master service run by Ash. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

Joining a game and how you are charged

  • To join a game you save a card on a secure payment page run by our payment provider, Whop. Your card details go to Whop, never to this website, and nothing is charged when you save the card.
  • You are charged the session price shown for that game (for example $10) at the start time of each session you have not skipped, for as long as you are in the game.
  • You must be 18 or older to book.
  • You can see your card, your upcoming sessions and your charges on your player page at any time.

Skipping and leaving

  • You can skip any upcoming session, or leave the game, up to 24 hours before it starts. Skipped sessions are not charged.
  • Inside 24 hours, message Ash. Late changes are at Ash's discretion.
  • If you leave, you are not charged for any session after you leave. You can rejoin later if there is a seat.

Refunds

  • If you were charged for a session you should not have been, or the session was cancelled by Ash, you will be refunded.
  • To ask for a refund, message Ash on Discord. Refunds go back to the card you paid with.

Declined cards

If a charge is declined we will email you and try again a few times over the next days. You can update your card from your player page. If it still cannot be charged, Ash may pause your seat until it is sorted out.

Privacy

  • We keep your account email, the display name you chose, which games you joined, which sessions you skipped, and a record of your charges and your consent to be charged.
  • Your display name and token are shown to the other players in your game. Your email is not.
  • We do not store your card number. Whop stores it and shows us only the card brand and the last four digits.
  • We do not sell your information. To have your data removed, message Ash.

Questions

Message Ash on Discord and it gets sorted out.
