Ravenloft: Undead Survival

The dead don't rest in the Domains of Dread. Out here, they don't even stay down.

Somewhere past the borders of the world you know, the Mists have claimed another village — and this one is drowning in its own graveyard. The dead have stopped staying buried, supplies are running out faster than anyone can ration them, and the only law left is: don't get bitten, don't get caught outside after dark, and don't trust anyone who's been gone too long.

You didn't ask to end up here. Nobody does. The Mists don't send invitations — they send verdicts.

The World

This corner of Ravenloft's endless Domains of Dread is ruled less by a single tyrant than by attrition itself. Every barricade you build buys a little time. Every supply run costs something. The horde outside isn't the kind you fight in a single glorious battle — it's the kind you survive, day by grinding day, hoping the walls hold and the food doesn't run out first.

What's at Stake

Somewhere behind the horde's endless hunger is the will of a Darklord — one of the countless damned rulers cursed to preside over their own personal nightmare forever. Break their hold, or at least understand it, and there might be a way out. Fail, and you become one more name added to the village's very long list of the missing.

Why This Table

This is gritty, resource-scarce survival horror — think carefully about every bullet, every bandage, every door you barricade. It rewards players who like tension, hard choices, and the creeping dread of hearing something scratch at the door long after you thought it was secured. If Curse of Strahd is a slow gothic tragedy, this is its faster, hungrier cousin.

Ration your supplies. Barricade the windows. The horde is patient — you might not have the luxury of being.
