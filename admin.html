💰 Getting paid

Players pay for your games straight into your own account on Whop, our payment partner. Ash Tabletop's 5% is taken automatically on each payment, and Whop's own fees apply. To receive money, Whop needs to check who you are and where to send it. You do that on Whop's secure pages, and we never see your bank or card details.
